STANDARD RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on this 1st day of August, 2026, by and between Oakridge Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Mercer ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND OCCUPANCY
The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the residential apartment located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield. The premises shall be occupied solely as a private dwelling for residential purposes by the named Tenant.

2. LEASE TERM
The term of this lease shall commence on September 1, 2026, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis or terminate on August 31, 2027, unless renewed in writing by both parties.

3. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
(a) The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly base rent of $1,650.00, due in advance on the first calendar day of each month.
(b) A late fee of $50.00 shall apply if rent is not received by the fifth calendar day of the month.
(c) The Tenant shall provide a refundable security deposit of $1,650.00 prior to move-in.

4. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
The Landlord shall provide water, sewer service, and municipal trash collection. The Tenant shall be responsible for establishing accounts and paying all charges for electricity, gas, internet, and heating services.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant covenants to maintain the dwelling in a clean, sanitary, and tenantable condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall promptly report any plumbing leaks, electrical failures, or structural defects to the Landlord.

6. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
The Landlord or Landlord's authorized agents may enter the premises during reasonable business hours upon giving at least 24 hours prior written notice to inspect the premises or make necessary repairs.

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the initial term by providing at least 30 days written notice to the other party.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Lease Agreement as of the date first above written.
