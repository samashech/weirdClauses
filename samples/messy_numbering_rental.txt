RESIDENTIAL TENANCY CONTRACT

This Tenancy Contract is entered into on December 15, 2026 by Maple Leaf Rentals and Morgan Bailey.

Section 1. Premises and Duration
The Landlord leases the residential property at 88 Elm Street. The lease commences on January 1, 2027 and runs for twelve calendar months.

Article II: Financial Arrangements
(a) The monthly rental fee is $1,350.00, payable on the first day of each calendar month.
(b) A deposit of $1,350.00 is required upon signing.
Late payments after day seven incur a late fee of 5%.

The Tenant shall maintain reasonable peace and quiet, respecting neighboring occupants at all hours of the day and night.

3.1.2 Utility Allocation and Maintenance
The Landlord shall pay for building trash and cold water. Tenant agrees to pay for electricity and winter heating fuel directly to the suppliers.

IV. Keys and Alterations
No locks shall be changed without the Landlord's explicit prior written consent. Tenant shall not paint interior walls bright neon shades.

V. Termination
Either party may terminate the tenancy with 60 days written notice prior to the expiration date.
